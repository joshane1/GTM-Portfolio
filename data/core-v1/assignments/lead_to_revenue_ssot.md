Required controlled outputs: 24 April MQL inquiries, 20 people, six SQLs, three sourced opportunities, one won by June 30, $940,000 new-business open pipeline at April 30, $360,000 April new-business bookings, and $70,000 April service revenue. Preserve raw records, historical stages, and data-quality exceptions. Document definitions and demonstrate row-level reconciliation. The method, SQL models, tests, and presentation are the engineer's work.
