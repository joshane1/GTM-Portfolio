# Fiction Cloud Inc.

Fiction Cloud Inc. is a fictional B2B workflow-software company with inbound, sales-led, and self-service trial motions. The core source records cover 2025-01-01 through 2026-06-30. All people, organizations, email addresses, and results are synthetic.

CRM owns operational accounts, contacts, opportunities, and stage history. Marketing owns inquiries and touches. Finance owns subscriptions and monthly service revenue. The identity table maps billing customers to CRM accounts. USD is the only currency in core v1.

The April SSOT case intentionally contains different MQL grains, renewal and duplicate pipeline rows, and bookings mislabeled as revenue. See reports/baseline and the source specification for agreed definitions.
